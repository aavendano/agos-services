# Commercial Potential Assessment Methodology

This document formalizes the criteria used by AA Digital Business to evaluate acquired and proprietary technological assets.

---

## Dimension Breakdown

### 1. Market Attractiveness (Weight: 25%)
- **TAM / SAM / SOM:** Size and accessibility of target addressable market.
- **CAGR:** Underlying industry market growth velocity.
- **Competitive Density:** Monopolistic advantage vs crowded red ocean.

### 2. Defensibility & IP Moat (Weight: 20%)
- **Proprietary Algorithms:** Patents, deterministic solvers, unique domain logic.
- **Switching Costs:** Workflow integration depth into customer ERP/production pipeline.
- **Technical Complexity:** Engineering barrier to replicate.

### 3. Financial Unit Economics (Weight: 20%)
- **LTV:CAC Ratio:** Target $\ge 3.0	imes$ (Elite $\ge 5.0	imes$).
- **Payback Period:** Target $\le 12$ months.
- **Gross Margin:** Target $\ge 80\%$.

### 4. Technology & Operational Maturity (Weight: 15%)
- **TRL (1-9):** Technology Readiness Level (validated prototypes in operational environments).
- **CRL (1-9):** Commercial Readiness Level (paying pilot customer readiness).
- **Technical Debt Penalty:** Refactoring effort required before commercial launch.

### 5. GTM Velocity (Weight: 10%)
- **Sales Cycle Duration:** Friction of sales process.
- **Onboarding Effort:** Time-to-value for end users.

### 6. Risk-Adjusted Viability (Weight: 10%)
- **Regulatory & Compliance Safety:** Freedom to operate under AI/data regulations.
